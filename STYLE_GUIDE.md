# FRIC LAW – 微信公众号中文内容风格指南

Derived from the user-provided Planning.docx, week1.1.docx, week1.2.docx, week1.3.docx, and week1.4.docx (October 2026). These reference drafts are **style examples, not verified legal authority**.

## Editorial voice
- Chinese simplified (简体中文), natural, clear, professional and trusted; like a Vietnam-practicing business lawyer briefing China-based investors.
- Prefer concrete advice over dense legal exposition. Show the difference between: 事实 / 法律规定 / 律师分析 / 商业机会.
- Avoid excessive exclamation marks or clickbait. Project posts may be more engaging, but do not overpromise returns, legal entitlements or project participation.
- Explain Vietnam concepts accurately (投资登记证书 IRC, 企业登记证书 ERC, 受益所有人 UBO, 直接投资资本账户 DICA). Maintain original Vietnamese enactment numbers and names.
- No claim of first-hand FRIC LAW experience or client success unless separately documented.

## Content pillars
1. 越南法律资讯: investment-entry rules, laws and amendments, decrees, bank regulations, capital remittances, tax, labor, corporate compliance.
2. 越南律师解答: actionable investor FAQs, corporate formation, equity ownership, market entry, M&A due diligence, foreign-element disputes.
3. 项目与投资机会: new industrial parks, energy, transport, urban infrastructure, finance; end with what Chinese suppliers/investors should investigate and which approvals matter.

## Structure, adapt to topic
- Clear headline: audience + change/event + meaningful effect. No fabricated claims.
- Lead: who did what, where, when, document number / project scale only when verified.
- 一、背景与最新进展 (what changed, with dates).
- 二、核心内容与适用范围 (facts, current law or project attributes).
- 三、对中资企业的影响 (transaction structure, costs, timelines, compliance, opportunity).
- 四、律师建议 / 实务提示 (concrete next steps, limitations, eligibility and risk).
- 结语: 2–3 factual sentences, restrained CTA where useful.
- 免责声明: general informational content, not legal advice for any particular client.
- 段红（Hong Doan）｜FRIC LAW 创始人、越南律师。
- 参考来源: all original source URLs, document titles, issue dates, access date.

## Evidence and source rules
- Apply STRICT 7-day window for NEW news by **original publication date**, not search indexing or article modification date. Unknown dates = awaiting verification. An older legal instrument may be referenced as BACKGROUND if still current; label it clearly rather than claiming it was issued this week.
- Prefer primary legal source (vanban.chinhphu.vn, congbao.chinhphu.vn, national legal DB, ministry or SBV) for precise claims, and crosscheck amendments, replacements, transitional provisions and effective dates against current texts. Press stories are discovery evidence, not authority for legal interpretation.
- Preserve amounts, percentages, dates, proper nouns and legal article numbers exactly; if unverified, write [待核实]. Don't invent any of them.
- An article must cite [S1], [S2], etc. right after the claim and list original URLs at the end. The text must not be copied at length from a news article.
- One topic per article. Avoid generic repetitive praise, exaggerated forecasts and excessive closing paragraphs.
